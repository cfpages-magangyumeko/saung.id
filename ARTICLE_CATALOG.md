# Article Catalog — saung.id

## Scope and domain boundaries

This catalog expands the complete saung/gazebo knowledge map into mutually distinct Indonesian article briefs. Each article owns one reader job. It may acknowledge related systems, but it must send structural, foundation, connection, chemical, fire, electrical, pest, and high-risk construction decisions to the named specialist article or competent professional.

Commercial service and location routes are outside this catalog. A location page is justified only by substantive local regulation, climate, supply, or documented project evidence; changing a place name is not a new article. Similar coverage on another owned domain is allowed because anti-cannibalization applies within `saung.id`.

## Catalog statistics

- Parent topics: 18
- Planned articles: 108
- Articles per topic: 6
- First bounded wave: 12 P0 assets
- Existing location-template URLs are consolidation inputs, not planned editorial articles.

## Priority legend

- `P0`: Wave 1 foundation needed to establish the entity, decision path, evidence standard, and useful lead context.
- `P1`: Important system, safety, selection, or lifecycle page that completes the next connected cluster.
- `P2`: Supporting depth after its hub and evidence workflow exist.
- `P3`: Specialist, historical, cultural, or lower-frequency depth that still completes the knowledge space.

## First-wave rationale and measurement

Wave 1 contains the 12 P0 assets named in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md`. They create one coherent path from entity definition and owner brief through site/layout, load path, foundation, material/roof selection, bid comparison, and preventive care. Every Wave 1 page links upward to its parent hub, laterally to the next decision, and back to `/saung.html`; the hub links to all 12.

Monitor valid indexation and canonical selection, impressions by distinct intent, worksheet/checklist use, meaningful engagement, qualified enquiries containing site/use/material facts, crawl behavior of legacy duplicate groups, and Search Console query-to-page splits. Ranking without task completion, evidence review, and conversion quality is insufficient.
